Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Birthday is a personal reminder app for birthdays, anniversaries and other important dates. We aim to make it clear how the app handles information.

Information you enter

Birthday lets you enter information such as names, dates of birth, relationships, anniversaries, notes and gift ideas. This information is used to provide the app's features.

Contacts

If you choose to import contacts, Birthday requests permission to access contacts on your device for that purpose. Contact access is optional; you can add people manually instead.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Birthday uses information in the app to schedule reminders about upcoming dates. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

Backup and restore

Birthday provides backup and restore features. If you export or share a backup file, where that file is saved or sent depends on the destination you choose. Treat backup files as private because they may contain personal information.

Data storage and third-party services

Children's information

The app may be used to record birthdays of family members, including children. Please only enter information you have a legitimate reason to keep.
